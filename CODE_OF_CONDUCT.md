# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct — Version 2.1

> 🇧🇬 Правила за уважително и безопасно участие в общността на BGDB AI-EMDS.

## Our Pledge

> 🇧🇬 Участието трябва да бъде достъпно и без тормоз за всички.

We pledge to make participation in BGDB AI-EMDS a harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity or orientation. We commit to a welcoming, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

> 🇧🇬 Насърчаваме конструктивния диалог и не допускаме обиди, заплахи и тормоз.

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for different views, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and professionalism. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized attention or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, harassment, sharing private information without permission, intimidation and threats.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

> 🇧🇬 Поддържащите проекта прилагат правилата справедливо и последователно.

Maintainers clarify and enforce standards. They may remove, edit or reject contributions or comments that violate this policy, explaining moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

> 🇧🇬 Правилата важат за GitHub и при официално представяне на проекта.

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and all other project spaces, including when a person officially represents BGDB AI-EMDS elsewhere.

## Reporting

> 🇧🇬 Изпращайте сигналите поверително до info@bgdb.eu.

Report unacceptable conduct privately to **info@bgdb.eu**, including context, links and evidence. Maintainers will review reports fairly and respect the privacy of participants. Do not file public issues that contain sensitive personal information.

## Enforcement Guidelines

> 🇧🇬 Мерките зависят от тежестта и повторяемостта на нарушението.

Based on Contributor Covenant 2.1, maintainers may use the following graduated responses:

1. **Correction:** private written warning for a minor first incident.
2. **Warning:** official warning with explicit boundaries for serious or repeated behavior.
3. **Temporary ban:** time-limited suspension for a serious violation.
4. **Permanent ban:** exclusion for severe or repeated misconduct threatening community safety.
